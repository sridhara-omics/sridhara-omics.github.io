## Solutions Architect | Data and AI Platforms | Life Sciences | Cloud

I design data and AI solutions that help science-driven and regulated-health organizations turn complex data into dependable,
decision-ready outcomes. With 10+ years across biotech, consulting and federal organizations, including NIH and FDA, I cover the full
data lifecycle, from ingestion and quality control to analytics and delivery.  

My roots in engineering and computational sciences let me work with both the scientists asking the questions and the engineers building the platforms that answer them.  

## What I do

- **Data architecture and engineering:** scalable platforms, ETL/ELT and workflow orchestration (Databricks, Nextflow, Snakemake).
- **Cloud:** Azure , AWS, Databricks, Docker and CI/CD.
- **AI and ML:** PyTorch, TensorFlow and MLflow, including protein language models such as ESM2.
- **Life sciences:** multi-omics, antibody repertoires, single-cell genomics and regulated data environments.

## Selected experience

- **Solutions Architect, Varmoda Technologies (2026 - present):** data, AI and cloud solutions for NIH, FDA and HHS missions.
- **Senior Bioinformatics Scientist, Argentys Informatics:** reproducible pipeline for antibody repertoire analysis on the Observed Antibody Space (2B+ sequences, about 3 TB), producing model-ready data for protein language models.
- **Bioinformatics Scientist (contract), Twist Bioscience:** zero-shot antibody fitness and developability inference with repeatable evaluation.
- **Senior Bioinformatics Scientist (contract), FDA:** bulk and single-cell RNA-seq pipelines.

Full history on my [CV](/cv/).
